🧠 Recommended Minimum Specs for Your Use Case

For smooth and future‑proof creative performance:

💻 Processor (Chip)

M4 Pro or M4 Max preferred — especially for video editing, multicam workflows, and heavy photo batches.

🧠 Unified Memory (RAM)

32 GB minimum — excellent for multitasking in Adobe apps and working with large Capture One catalogs.

Consider 48–64 GB if you’re pushing 4K/8K video or massive layered files.

💽 Storage

1 TB SSD minimum — scratch space and cache files in Premiere/After Effects eat storage fast.

2 TB or more if you work with large media natively.

🖥 Display

Liquid Retina XDR (on Pro models) for accurate colors — essential for photography and video.
